Definition 3

Let \(F \subseteq G\) be an inclusion of vector bundles on \(X\). Then there is a unique intermediate bundle \(F'\) such that \(\operatorname{rank}(F) = \operatorname{rank}(F')\) and \(G/F'\) is a vector bundle, called the saturation of \(F\) in \(G\). We then have \(\deg (F) \leq \deg (F')\) with equality if and only if \(F' = F\).

Definition 5

Let \(F\) be a nonzero vector bundle on \(X\). We define the slope of \(F\) as

\[ \mu (F) := \frac{\deg (F)}{\operatorname{rank}(F)}. \]

The bundle \(F\) is stable if there is no nonzero proper subbundle \(G\) of \(F\) with \(\mu (G) \geq \mu (F)\). The bundle \(F\) is semistable if there is no nonzero subbundle \(G\) of \(F\) with \(\mu (G) {\gt} \mu (F)\). The bundle \(F\) is polystable if it is a direct sum of stable vector bundles, all of the same slope.

Definition 8

For \(F\) a vector bundle on \(X\), the filtration on \(F\) described in ??prop:HN filtration is called the Harder–Narasimhan filtration, or HN filtration, of \(F\). We define the multiset of HN slopes of \(F\) to consist of \(\mu _i = \deg (F_i/F_{i-1})/\operatorname{rank}(F_i/F_{i-1})\) with multiplicity \(\operatorname{rank}(F_i/F_{i-1})\).

Theorem 25

Set

\[ S := \begin{cases} \mathbb {Z}& \mbox{in the classical case,} \\ \tfrac {1}{2} \mathbb {Z}& \mbox{in the twistor case,} \\ \mathbb {Q}& \mbox{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]

Then the following statements hold.

  1. There exists exactly one isomorphism class of stable vector bundles \(\mathcal{O}_X(\lambda )\) of slope \(\lambda \) if \(\lambda \in S\) and none if \(\lambda \notin S\).

  2. For \(\lambda \in S\) with denominator \(d\) in lower terms, \(\operatorname{rank}(\mathcal{O}_X(\lambda )) = d\).

  3. The endomorphisms of \(\mathcal{O}_X(\lambda )\) form a division algebra of degree \(d\) over \(P_0\).

  4. For \(\lambda , \lambda ' \in S\), \(\mathcal{O}_X(\lambda ) \otimes \mathcal{O}_X(\lambda ') \cong \mathcal{O}_X(\lambda +\lambda ')^{\oplus m}\) for some positive integer \(m\).

  5. Every vector bundle on \(X\) splits (nonuniquely) as a direct sum of summands each of the form \(\mathcal{O}_X(\lambda )\) for some \(\lambda \in S\).
